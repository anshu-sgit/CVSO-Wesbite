Who We Are

Through the support of the Cumberland Valley Science Olympiad Parent Boosters, our teams are able to participate in a variety of competitions throughout the year.

We strive to be a source of knowledge and inspiration for students all around our local community.

Sponsorship Levels

All contributions directly benefit CVSD student competitors. Each level includes every benefit of the level below it, plus its own.

Platinum
$5,000+

The ultimate level of community leadership and national-level support.

Includes all Gold benefits, plus:
  • Prominent Branding: Logo featured most prominently on the CVSO website, event programs, and Eagle Invitational T-shirts
  • Active Engagement: Exclusive speaking opportunity at the award ceremony or the honor of presenting tournament trophies
  • Dedicated Recognition: Individual social media spotlight and dedicated thank-you post
  • Lasting Commemoration: Custom team photo plaque and certificate of appreciation
Gold
$2,500 – $4,999

Directly funds an entire tournament's medals, test materials, and supplies.

Includes all Silver benefits, plus:
  • Large-Scale Visibility: Large logo on the CVSO website and Eagle Invitational T-shirts
  • Dual Recognition: Mentioned during both the Eagle Invitational award ceremony and the CVSO End of Year Celebration
  • Formal Award: Certificate of appreciation
Silver
$1,000 – $2,499

Your first opportunity for mobile brand exposure via T-shirts. Funds lab kits, building materials, and competition tools.

Includes all Bronze benefits, plus:
  • T-Shirt Branding: Medium logo on the official Eagle Invitational T-shirts worn by participants
  • Increased Web Presence: Medium logo on the CVSO Booster Club website
  • Social Media: Inclusion in a group thank-you post across social platforms
Bronze
$500 – $999

Provides essential support for snacks, team apparel, and volunteer appreciation.

Includes all Community benefits, plus:
  • Visual Branding: Small logo or name featured on the CVSO Booster Club website
  • Program Recognition: Business officially recognized within the event programs
  • Ceremony Mention: Specific mention in the closing ceremony program
Community
$100 – $499

Foundational support for printing, safety materials, and student travel.

  • Website Listing: Name listed on the CVSO Booster Club website
  • Social Media: Included in a social media thank-you
  • On-Site Display: Option to display promotional material at the event **
In-Kind
Value Varies

Recognition tailored to the value of donated goods or services (food, printing, lab materials, etc.).

  • Logo or name on the CVSO Booster Club website and event materials
  • Recognition level matched to the value of the donation

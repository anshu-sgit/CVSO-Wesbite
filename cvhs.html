About the Team

Varsity STEM at Cumberland Valley

The Cumberland Valley High School Science Olympiad team competes in Division C (grades 9–12) against the top programs in central Pennsylvania. Our members specialize across the full slate of 23 events — from Anatomy & Physiology and Chemistry Lab to Flight, Towers, and Electric Vehicle.

Students typically pair into event partnerships, mentored by upperclassmen and coaches, and train through the fall and winter toward the Regional and State tournaments in the spring. Beyond medals, the team is a tight-knit community where students build lasting friendships and discover future college majors and careers.

The high school team also mentors our two middle school teams and helps run the annual Eagle Invitational.

Our Schedule

How Practices Run

Practices run Tuesday through Friday, from after school until 5:00 PM. During practice, members take practice tests, construct and test their builds, and prepare for their lab and study events.

Practice Tests

Members take practice tests to sharpen their knowledge and get ready for competition-day conditions.

Build & Test

Build teams construct and test their devices, refining designs through trial and iteration.

Labs & Study

Members practice hands-on lab skills, study their events, and work closely with their event partners.

Event Categories

What We Compete In

The 2027 Division C slate spans five categories:

Life, Personal & Social Science

Anatomy & Physiology, Botany, Designer Genes, Disease Detectives, Water Quality.

Earth & Space Science

Astronomy, Dynamic Planet, Remote Sensing, Rocks and Minerals.

Physical Science & Chemistry

Chemistry Lab, Circuit Lab, Forensics, Hovercraft, Protein Modeling, Thermodynamics.

Technology & Engineering

Boomilever, Electric Vehicle, Mission Possible, Wright Stuff.

Inquiry & Nature of Science

Codebusters, Engineering CAD, Experimental Design, Ping-Pong Parachute.

Note: The official event list changes each year. See the full 2027 lineup at soinc.org 2027 event table.

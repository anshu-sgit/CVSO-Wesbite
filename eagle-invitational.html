About the Tournament

Eagle Invitational — For B Division Teams

The 9th Annual Eagle Invitational Science Olympiad competition is on Saturday, February 13, 2027 at Mountain View Middle School. Run by our coaches and high school students, the Invitational welcomes Division B (middle school) teams from across the Northeast Science Olympiad regions — including Cumberland Valley's own Mountain View and Eagle View teams.

All rules for Science Olympiad competitions apply. We run the full slate of 23 official Division B events, and all lab materials, testing rigs, and written tests are provided by us — participating coaches are not required to write and prepare tests. All tests and score sheets are made available to every team at the end of the competition.

Competition

Events & Requirements

Events

We will run the full slate of 23 official Division B events. Lab and hybrid events will include hands-on components as described in the Division B Rules Manual. All events are run, judged, and scored using regional level rules.

Requirements

Each participating school must provide a volunteer to act as an event supervisor or event judge. All lab materials, testing rigs, and written tests will be provided by us — participating coaches will not be required to write and prepare tests. All tests and score sheets will be shared with all teams at the end of the competition.

Scilympiad

Students take their tests on paper, but we use our Scilympiad site as our tournament scoring management system.

  • Student participants will not use Scilympiad to compete
  • Coaches will not populate rosters or events with students in Scilympiad
  • Coaches will not submit forms through Scilympiad
  • Coaches use Scilympiad for self-scheduling
  • Raw scores are entered by Event Supervisors
  • Team rankings are tabulated and released to coaches through Scilympiad

Please Note

Tournament Policies

Official FAQs and rule clarifications published by NSO on soinc.org also apply to the Eagle Invitational. Tournament-specific FAQs are posted as needed; our intent is to remain consistent with all official rules and to present each event as described in the Division B Rules Manual.

Photos & Video

Do not record video or take photos of students and their devices without the consent of the student's parent or coach.

Common areas and classrooms must be kept clean. Please eat in the cafeteria.

Home Bases

Home bases must be supervised at all times.

Rules Level

Events will be run at the invitational/regional level rules.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How many students can be on a roster for Eagle Invy?

You may have up to 15 participants.

Will wristbands be issued?

Each registered roster will receive 15 official wristbands. Students are required to wear a wristband to compete in an official event. Wristbands are not required for trial events, if held.

Will we use the PASO or the NSO Experimental Design rubric?

Because we have teams coming from out-of-state, we will use the rubric published on soinc.org.

Will there be any trial events?

No.

What paperwork is required from coaches before or upon arrival?

Coaches are required only to sign in at the welcome desk upon arrival. We will not be collecting forms from coaches this year.

Will there be food?

Yes. There will be a concession stand for the students and a hospitality lounge for coaches, volunteers, and bus drivers.

What rules level will be used?

All events will be run, judged, and scored using the regional level rules.
